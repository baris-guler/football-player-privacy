Privacy at a Glance

  • You do not need to create an ATEA Games account to play.

  • We use analytics to understand how the App is used and improve gameplay.
  • Advertising providers may process advertising identifiers and ad interaction information.
  • Crash and technical diagnostic information may be collected to fix errors.

  • Purchases are processed by the relevant app store. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Consent and advertising preferences can be managed through the App or your device settings where available.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on how you use the App, your device settings, your consent choices, and the services available in your country.

2.1 Device and Technical Information

We or our service providers may process technical information such as:

  • Device manufacturer and model
  • Operating system and operating system version
  • Device platform
  • App version
  • Unity version
  • Device and system language
  • Current game scene
  • Date and time of events
  • App installation or instance identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • Internet Protocol address, which may be processed by service providers
  • Approximate or coarse location inferred from an IP address
  • Network, request, and diagnostic information
  • Crash logs, error messages, and stack traces

The App does not request or collect precise GPS location through a device location permission.

2.2 Gameplay and Analytics Information

We collect gameplay events to understand how the App is used, balance the game, identify problems, and improve the player experience.

This information may include:

  • Character creation activity
  • Selected in-game character nationality
  • Selected league country
  • Selected difficulty
  • Career or save slot number
  • Day progression
  • Match results
  • Home and away scores
  • Goals and assists
  • Player match rating
  • Completed training activities
  • Training type and skill gains
  • Advertising request, display, click, and error events

An in-game character’s selected nationality represents a fictional game attribute. It is not intended to describe the user’s actual nationality, race, or ethnicity.

2.3 Advertising Information

The App may display advertisements through third-party advertising providers.

Depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices, advertising providers may process:

  • Advertising ID on Android
  • Identifier for Advertisers, also known as IDFA, on iOS if permission is granted
  • App installation or device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Ad requests and impressions
  • Ad clicks and other interactions
  • Ad type and placement
  • Ad loading or display errors
  • Advertising performance and attribution information
  • Paid ad value, currency, and reporting precision
  • Consent and privacy preference signals

Advertising providers may combine information collected through the App with information collected from other apps, websites, or services in accordance with their own privacy policies.

2.4 Crash Reports and Automatic Error Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics and limited automatic error reporting to identify crashes, freezes, and technical problems.

Crash and error information may include:

  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • App and Unity versions
  • Game and system language
  • Current scene
  • Current match score
  • Match rating
  • Player’s in-game team
  • In-game date
  • Error message
  • Stack trace
  • Crash or installation identifier

Automatic error reports are sent only for technical troubleshooting. They are not intended to include your name, email address, custom player image, or payment information.

2.5 Bug Reports and Support Communications

The App allows you to voluntarily create a bug report or suggestion through your device’s email application.

The App may automatically prepare technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Game language
  • System language

You decide whether to send the email and may review or edit its contents before sending it.

If you contact us, we may receive:

  • Your email address and display name
  • Email subject and message
  • Technical information included in the message
  • Email signature
  • Screenshots, saved files, or other attachments you choose to include

Support emails are processed through your email provider and Google’s Gmail or Google Workspace services.

Please do not include passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, health information, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information in a bug report or support message.

2.6 Purchases and Subscription Information

The App may offer monthly or yearly subscriptions or other in-app purchases where supported by the relevant platform and release.

Payments are processed by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not directly process, receive, or store your payment card or bank account details.

The App may receive limited purchase information required to provide the purchased feature, such as:

  • Product or subscription identifier
  • Purchase status
  • Subscription entitlement status
  • Transaction or receipt validation information
  • Renewal, cancellation, or expiration status

Purchase availability may vary depending on the platform, country, App version, and store configuration.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is generally stored locally on your device:

  • Career and game save data
  • Game progress
  • Player and career settings
  • Custom player images selected from your device
  • Imported identity packs or game customization files
  • Audio, control, graphics, and in-game camera settings
  • Local purchase entitlement or advertising-removal status

ATEA Games does not intentionally upload your career save, custom player image, or imported identity pack to its analytics or advertising providers.

This information may leave your device only when you deliberately export, share, or attach it to a support message.

Apple or Google device backup services may include locally stored App data depending on your device and backup settings. Those backups are controlled by the relevant platform provider.

The App does not require access to your device microphone. References to a “camera” within game settings refer to the in-game football camera and not the device’s physical camera.

7. Analytics and Google Signals

On Android, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand App usage and improve Football Player. Google Analytics Advertising Features, including Google Signals, may be used only where applicable consent and platform settings allow them.

On iOS and iPadOS, Firebase Analytics collection and Google Analytics advertising-personalization signals are disabled. Firebase Crashlytics remains enabled for crash reports and technical diagnostics, but it is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

This may provide aggregated reports about:

  • Approximate age ranges
  • Gender
  • General interests
  • Cross-device usage patterns
  • Advertising and audience performance

Reports made available to ATEA Games are aggregated and are not intended to directly identify individual users.

You can control Google advertising personalization through your Google Account or device advertising settings.

Google Analytics Retention

For Android analytics data, our current Google Analytics retention settings are:

  • Event data: 2 months
  • User data: 14 months
  • Reset retention period on new activity: Enabled

Age, gender, and interest information associated with Google Signals is retained for up to 2 months, regardless of other user-property retention settings.

Google Analytics retention settings generally apply to user-level and event-level data. Aggregated standard reports may be retained for longer because they are not affected by the same retention control.

Google Analytics Data Retention information: support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196

10. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Analytics Data

The following retention periods apply to Android analytics data. Firebase Analytics collection is disabled on iOS and iPadOS.

  • Google Analytics event data is retained for 2 months.
  • Google Analytics user data is retained for 14 months.
  • The user-data retention period resets when new activity occurs.
  • Google Signals age, gender, and interest information is retained for up to 2 months.
  • Aggregated reports may be retained for longer.

Crash Reports

Firebase Crashlytics generally retains crash information for 90 days before the removal process begins, subject to Firebase’s service operation and policies.

Bug Reports and Support Messages

Bug reports and support correspondence are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate, respond to, and resolve the relevant issue.

They are periodically reviewed and deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

Information may be retained for longer where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, prevent abuse, meet tax or accounting obligations, or comply with applicable law.

Local Game Data

Locally stored game data generally remains on your device until you:

  • Delete it through an available in-game option
  • Clear the App’s data
  • Delete the relevant file
  • Uninstall the App

Copies may remain in platform-controlled device backups according to your Apple or Google backup settings.
